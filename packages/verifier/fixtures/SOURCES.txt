DRAWPROOF VERIFIER FIXTURES: SOURCES AND LICENCES

What is real and what is not
- The PHOTOGRAPHS are real, published under CC0 or in the public domain (listed below). They were
  resized to 1280 px wide (fixtures/source/), some cropped, and re-stamped with FIXTURE EXIF (capture
  time and GPS) by scripts/make-fixtures.ts so they read as taken at the fictional Maple St site.
  The EXIF on every output photo is therefore synthetic, and none of these photos shows Maple St.
- Everything else is SYNTHETIC and labelled as such: the builder, project, invoices, lien waiver,
  footing sign-off, licence and insurance documents, the inspectors (fixtures/inspectors.json,
  names fictional, MOCK), the inspector audits, and the audit txHash values (fake, derived from a
  hash of the file path; they are not on any ledger).
- Oak Ave is a fictional earlier project. Its photo is the source of the 'reused photo' scenario.
