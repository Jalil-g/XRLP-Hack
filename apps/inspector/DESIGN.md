# Inspector workspace design

The user's 2026-10-25 full frontend redesign supersedes the earlier Apple/Notion direction. Follow [the shared frontend guide](../../docs/ideas/shared-frontend-style.md).

The queue prioritizes work requiring an audit, re-audit, or site visit. Audits separate evidence review from report composition while retaining both views' state. The final review remains mandatory before signing. Signing/submission motion follows the real phase; the success seal appears only after filing succeeds.

Keep all evidence-version checks, browser key handling, assigned-inspector enforcement, and submitted-report receipts intact. Signing animation never claims that funds were released.
