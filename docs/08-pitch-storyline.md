## 3. Narration spine

1. **Intro:** “We're Drawproof. Construction money should move only when the building does.”
2. **Problem:** More than 800 investors put approximately $62.8 million into the Nightingale/CrowdStreet deals. The sponsor diverted funds. Use the case as motivation for accountability, not a claim that Drawproof would prevent the entire fraud. Bridge to the construction problem: lenders need evidence of progress before the next draw.
3. **Solution:** AI reviews evidence consistency, an inspector signs a site audit, and XRPL provides the funding and escrow record. The platform release service controls release timing.
4. **Flow:** Investors fund a project, its milestones are locked in escrow, evidence is reviewed, and a milestone is released or held.
5. **Demo:** Follow the [recording script](04-demo-script.md). Keep the project and milestone visible, show review before signing or paying, and end with the failed milestone still locked.
6. **Close:** “We earn verification, origination, and management fees. We're looking for one construction-lending pilot. Thank you.”

Primary case source: [DOJ sentencing release](https://www.justice.gov/usao-ndga/pr/head-commercial-real-estate-investment-firm-sentenced-federal-prison-628-million-fraud). Use “$62.8M,” not the older rounded “$63M,” in deck copy.

## 4. Claims and visual boundaries

- **AI:** evidence-consistency review; duplicate-photo matching is deterministic. Current Q&A/verifier docs describe mock AI judgments and unvalidated live vision. Label the actual recording mode. Claim a live model only with evidence from the recorded run.
- **Inspector:** signs a site audit. Do not imply a hardware wallet or autonomous AI site inspection.
- **XRPL:** escrow destination and expiry are ledger rules. The platform release service holds preimages and controls timing. The ledger does not evaluate an AI verdict. Do not imply broker borrower selection is constrained on-chain end to end.
- **Environment:** devnet prototype; mock RLUSD IOU is not production RLUSD. Show real transaction links only for actual transactions. Label fixtures/fake ledger and mocked checks clearly.
- **Omit the old “Ledger says no” pitch beat:** rejection of a wrong-secret finish does not prove that the ledger enforces AI checks. Retain the test as engineering evidence with its actual scope.
- **Design:** calm white canvas, restrained blue actions, readable type, editable diagrams, and explicit status words. No decorative cards, gradients, glass, generic AI imagery, or invented metrics. Use the shared guide across frontend, deck, and video.
